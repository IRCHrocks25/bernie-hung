The Source

Grown, researched and made by one company. From seed to jar.

Before I put my name to RIMAN, I checked these facts. You can check them too.

A protected plant

Giant BYoungPool™ is RIMAN’s own strain of Centella Asiatica. It is protected by Plant Variety Protection rights in Korea and the USA, with exclusive usage rights for 20 years.

Grown on Jeju Island

It grows on RIMAN FARM on Jeju Island, the world’s first Centella Asiatica Smart Farm, with mineral-rich water that has moved through Jeju’s volcanic rock for 400,000 years.

Built around delivery

RIMAN’s nanoliposomal technology is designed around how ingredients are delivered, not only what goes in the jar.

Tested for tolerance

The ICD Dermatology Cream, Booster and Serum were each rated “excellent” for skin tolerance by Dermatest® in Germany, in a four-week study under dermatological control.

Premium, without the luxury-house price

Serious formulation at an affordable-luxury price, so the routine is one you can keep up.

Also good to know

Meet Bernie

The lawyer who did the due diligence.

For 30 years I’ve worked in law, advising on intellectual property, regulation and risk. I spent eight years in global luxury, so I know the difference between a beautiful story and real substance.

I was also the person this page is written for. Long days, a cupboard of good products, and no idea which ones were worth it.

In January 2025 a health scare stopped me in my tracks, and my income dropped almost overnight. I saw the flaw in the business I’d built: it only worked when I did.

A friend from my luxury days introduced me to RIMAN. I wasn’t sold. So I did what lawyers do and investigated. I found protected plant rights in Korea and the US, RIMAN’s own Smart Farm on Jeju Island, and a business model attached to it. That’s why I’m here.

I’m originally from Hong Kong and now based in London. I work with customers and Business Builders across the UK, Europe, North America, Asia Pacific and Australia, in English, Cantonese and Mandarin.

Questions people ask me

Is RIMAN a pyramid scheme?

It was my first question too. Read RIMAN’s official materials, ask me anything, and decide for yourself. No income is guaranteed.

I already have loads of skincare.

Then the K-popup is for you. Bring what you use now and we’ll look at it together. You may need fewer products, not more.

No. It’s free and there’s no obligation.
